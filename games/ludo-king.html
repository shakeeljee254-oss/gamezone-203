Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about gameplay modes, controls, and local progress storage on GameZone203.

Is Ludo King – Ultimate Edition free to play?

Yes, you can launch and play Classic or Quick Ludo directly in your web browser without paying a fee or installing software.

What is the difference between Classic Ludo and Quick Ludo?

Classic Ludo requires bringing all 4 tokens to the center crown home, while Quick Ludo starts with 1 token deployed and lets the first player to guide 1 token home win faster.

Where is my profile, coin balance, and match save stored?

Your username, avatar, coins, XP, achievements, and active match save are stored in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off on the same device and browser.

Why are safe squares and exact rolls important?

Colored start tiles and star (★) squares protect tokens from capture, and you must roll the exact number needed to enter the center crown finish.

📜 Official Ludo Rules

1. Deploying Tokens (Rolling a 6) Roll a 6 to move a token from your home yard onto your colored starting square. Rolling a 6 also awards a bonus roll! (Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits the turn if enabled).
2. Movement & Capturing Opponents Tokens travel clockwise around the 52-square outer circuit. Landing on an opponent's single token on a non-safe square captures it, sending it back to its yard and granting you a bonus roll.
3. Safe Zones & Token Stacks Colored starting squares and Star (★) tiles are Safe Zones where no token can be captured. Multiple tokens of the same color can stack safely together.
4. Home Stretch & Winning After completing a full lap, tokens enter their colored 5-tile Home Column. An exact dice roll is required to enter the center crown triangle. Bring all 4 tokens home (or 1 in Quick Ludo) to win!
