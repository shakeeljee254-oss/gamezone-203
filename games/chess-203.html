Chess Pieces Names and How They Move

          
The six pieces are Pawn (1 square forward, 2 on start, captures diagonally), Rook (straight lines), Knight (L-shape), Bishop (diagonals), Queen (straight & diagonal), and King (1 square any direction).

        

        

          
How Does the Bishop Move in Chess?

          
The bishop in a chess game moves diagonally across open squares. Each player starts with two bishops, one on a light square and one on a dark square.

        

        

          
What Is a Draw in a Chess Game?

          
A draw happens when neither side can force checkmate, including stalemate, insufficient material, or threefold repetition.

        

        

          
Who Invented the Board Game Chess?

          
Chess originated in northern India around the 6th century CE as Chaturanga before spreading through Persia and Europe into the modern game.
