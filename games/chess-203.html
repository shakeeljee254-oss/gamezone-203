Is Chess 203 Free?

          
Yes, Chess 203 is completely free to play online directly in your web browser with no downloads required.

        


        
        

          
What is the name of the pieces in chess game?

          
The six pieces are Pawn, Knight, Bishop, Rook, Queen, and King. In Chess 203, white pieces are highlighted in cyan and black pieces in pink.

        

        

          
How does the bishop move in Chess 203?

          
The bishop in a chess game can only move diagonally. Each player starts with two bishops, one on light squares and one on dark squares.

        

        

          
What is a draw in a chess game?

          
A draw occurs when neither player can checkmate the opponent. Common draws include stalemate, insufficient material, and threefold repetition.
