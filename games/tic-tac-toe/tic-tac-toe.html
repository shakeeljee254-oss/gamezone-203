Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Tic Tac Toe?

Players take turns placing X or O on a 3×3 grid. The first to get three in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins.

Can I play Tic Tac Toe against the computer?

Yes. Choose from three AI difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard (unbeatable minimax AI).

Is this Tic Tac Toe game free?
