How to Play Metro Rush 3D

Dash along busy city tracks in Metro Rush 3D on GameZone 203. Switch lanes to avoid moving and parked trains, jump over hurdles, slide under high barriers, and trigger hoverboards when danger gets close.

⬅️ ➡️ Lane & Jump Controls

Use Left/Right arrows or A/D (or swipe horizontally) to switch lanes, and Up/W or swipe up to jump.

⬇️ 🛹 Slide & Hoverboard

Press Down/S or swipe down to slide, and press Spacebar or double-tap to activate your hoverboard.

⚡ Power-Ups & Upgrades

Grab magnets, jetpacks, shields, and 2x multipliers during runs, then spend coins on runners and upgrades.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Metro Rush 3D free to play on GameZone 203?

Yes, Metro Rush 3D runs directly in your browser with no download or registration required.

Yes, click the Night/Day toggle button in the top bar or inside the pause menu anytime.
