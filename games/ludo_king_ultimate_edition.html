How to Play Ludo King Ultimate & FAQ

Q: How do I deploy and move tokens? A: Click Roll Dice (or press Spacebar). Roll a 6 to move a token out of your home yard onto the starting square, then click any glowing token to advance.
Q: How do captures and safe zones work? A: Landing on an opponent's token outside a Star safe square captures it, sending it back to its yard and granting you a bonus roll!
