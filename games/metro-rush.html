Metro Rush 3D — Free Online Endless Runner Game

Metro Rush 3D is a fast-paced, free-to-play 3D endless runner game where you sprint through busy metro tracks, dodge oncoming trains, slide under barriers, and collect coins to unlock new characters and hoverboards. Play instantly in your browser — no download, no registration, and no payment required. Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

How to Play Metro Rush 3D

Metro Rush 3D is easy to learn but hard to master. Follow these simple steps to start your run:

  1. Start the game: Click the PLAY button on the main menu.
  2. Change lanes: Swipe left or right on mobile, or press the Left/Right arrow keys (or A/D) on keyboard.
  3. Jump over obstacles: Swipe up or press the Up arrow (or W or Space) to jump over barriers, crates, and low trains.
  4. Slide under barriers: Swipe down or press the Down arrow (or S) to slide under gates and high obstacles.
  5. Collect coins and power-ups: Grab gold coins to spend in the Shop, and pick up power-ups like Magnet, Jetpack, Shield, and 2× Score to boost your run.
  6. Pause anytime: Press P or Esc to pause the game.

Power-Ups in Metro Rush 3D

Metro Rush 3D features 7 unique power-ups that can turn a struggling run into a record-breaking one:

  • 🧲 Magnet: Attracts all nearby coins towards you for 10 seconds.
  • 🚀 Jetpack: Fly above obstacles and collect coins in the sky for 6 seconds.
  • 👟 Super Sneakers: Gives you a much higher and longer jump for 10 seconds.
  • ✖2 Score: Doubles all points earned for 10 seconds — perfect for high scores.
  • 🛹 Hoverboard: Protects you from one crash and lasts 20 seconds.
  • 🛡️ Shield: Blocks one hit and gives you brief invincibility.
  • ⚡ Speed Boost: Makes you run faster with temporary invincibility for 5 seconds.

Characters & Hoverboards

Earn coins during your runs and spend them in the Shop to unlock 5 unique characters — Kai, Mira, Zed, Luna, and Rex — each with their own style, accessories, and outfits. You can also unlock 4 hoverboard skins (Spark, Neon, Magma, Aurora) to ride in style.

Daily Missions & Achievements

Complete daily missions, weekly challenges, and achievement badges to earn bonus coins and XP. Log in every day to claim your daily reward — 7-day streaks give bigger and bigger coin bonuses (up to 600 coins).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I play Metro Rush 3D?
Use arrow keys or WASD on keyboard, or swipe on mobile to change lanes, jump, and slide. Dodge trains, barriers, and other obstacles. Collect coins and power-ups to boost your score and unlock new characters.
Can I play Metro Rush 3D on mobile?
Yes, Metro Rush 3D has full swipe controls for mobile devices. Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump, and swipe down to slide. The game works on both Android and iOS browsers.
Is Metro Rush 3D free to play?
Yes, Metro Rush 3D is completely free to play. No download, no registration, and no payment required. Just open the page and start playing instantly in your browser.
How do I unlock new characters in Metro Rush 3D?
Collect coins during your runs and use them in the Shop to unlock new characters and hoverboard skins. There are 5 characters and 4 hoverboard styles to unlock, each with unique looks.
What are power-ups in Metro Rush 3D?
Metro Rush 3D features 7 power-ups: Magnet (attracts coins), Jetpack (fly over obstacles), Super Sneakers (higher jumps), 2x Score (double points), Hoverboard (protection), Shield (one-time hit protection), and Speed Boost (faster running with invincibility).
How do I beat my high score in Metro Rush 3D?
Run as far as possible while collecting coins and power-ups. Use the 2x score power-up and stay on the hoverboard for protection. The longer you survive, the faster the game gets, and the more points you earn. Complete daily missions for bonus coins.
Does Metro Rush 3D save my progress?
Yes, Metro Rush 3D automatically saves your coins, best score, unlocked characters, missions, and settings in your browser's local storage. Your progress stays on the same device and browser.
