📖 How to Win 2048 & Core Rules

  • How to Win 2048: Slide matching numbered tiles together (2+2=4, up to 2048 and higher) while keeping one corner anchored for your highest tile.
  • High Score Strategy: Build descending values along a single row or column and avoid random upward/sideward swipes that trap lower tiles.
  • Undo & Power-Ups: Use Undo Move, Shuffle Tiles, or Remove Tile when the board gets tight.
