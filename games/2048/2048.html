📖 How to Play & Core Rules

  • Objective: Slide numbered tiles on the grid so matching numbers collide and merge into their sum (2 + 2 = 4, up to 2048 and beyond).
  • One Merge Per Move: Each tile can only merge once per directional swipe.
  • Pro Strategy Tip: Keep your highest-value tile anchored in one corner and build descending chains along an adjacent edge.
