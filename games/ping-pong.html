📖 How to Play

🖥️ Desktop Controls Single Player / Practice: W / S or Arrow Up / Arrow Down (or mouse drag).
Local 2 Player: Player 1 (Left) uses W / S • Player 2 (Right) uses ↑ / ↓.
Space: Pause / Resume • Esc: Open Pause Menu.
📱 Mobile & Touchscreen Controls Touch and drag vertically on your side of the table. In Local 2 Player, left half moves Player 1 and right half moves Player 2 at the same time.
🏓 Angles, Spin & Scoring Hitting near the top or bottom edge of your paddle angles the ball sharply, and moving your paddle adds spin. Matches are played to 11 points (must win by 2), alternating serves every 2 points.
