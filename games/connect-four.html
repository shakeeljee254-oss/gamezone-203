How to Play Connect Four 203

Connect Four 203 is a classic two-player vertical strategy game played on an upright 7-column by 6-row grid. Players take turns dropping colored discs (Red for Player 1, Yellow for Player 2 or the Computer AI) from the top into any non-full column.

  • Gravity Drop: Each disc falls straight down to occupy the lowest empty row inside the chosen column.
  • Winning Objective: Be the first player to form an unbroken line of four discs of your color horizontally, vertically, or diagonally (up-right or down-right).
  • Draw Condition: If all 42 slots are filled without either player connecting four discs, the round ends in a draw.

Controls & Winning Strategy

Desktop & Mobile Controls: Click or tap any column directly on the board to drop a disc. On keyboards, use ← / → arrows with Enter or press number keys 1–7.

Control the Center Column: Column 4 (the middle column) is part of the highest number of horizontal and diagonal four-in-a-row combinations. Securing central positions early creates double-threat traps that force your opponent into losing moves.

AI Difficulty Levels: Easy plays casually for beginners; Medium actively blocks three-in-a-row threats; Hard uses a deep Minimax search with Alpha-Beta pruning and positional evaluation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play Connect Four 203 with a friend on the same device? ▼

Yes! Select 2 Players in the Match Setup panel to alternate Red and Yellow turns on the same phone, tablet, or computer. Both players' wins and daily quests are tracked cleanly.

How does the Hard AI work? ▼

On Hard difficulty, the computer evaluates future board states using a 6-ply Minimax algorithm with Alpha-Beta pruning, center-column weighting, immediate win detection, and forced threat blocking.

Are my wins, quests, and streaks saved automatically? ▼

Yes. Your wins, draws, total matches, daily quests, achievements, and best streak are saved locally in your browser's localStorage under an isolated Connect Four key without affecting other GameZone203 games.
