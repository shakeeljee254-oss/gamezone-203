How to Play Connect Four 203

Drop Red or Yellow discs into the 7×6 grid. Discs fall to the lowest open row in that column. Align four discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.

Controls & AI Levels

Tap/click any column or press 1–7 on your keyboard. Choose Easy for casual play, Medium for threat blocking, or Hard for deep tactical search.
