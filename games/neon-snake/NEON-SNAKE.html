HOW TO PLAY

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD, Space or P to pause. On mobile swipe the arena or use the arrows.
Arena: the edges wrap around. You are eliminated by hitting your own body, another snake's body, or a barrier.
Head-on: the longer snake wins; equal length eliminates both.
Fruit: Apple 10, Banana 20, Orange 30, Grape 40, Golden 100. Every 100 points is a new level: barriers appear from level 3 and a boss snake arrives every 5 levels (survive 30 s for +300).
Items: Speed, Slow, Shield (one hit), Magnet, Double Score, Freeze Blast (slows nearby AI).
Modes: Battle (last alive), Time (60 s), Speed Battle, Survival (AI respawns and gets tougher).
